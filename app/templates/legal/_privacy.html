1. Who we are

{{ app_name }} is a free GATE practice website run by its two founders, Surajit Sadhukhan and Sayan Karmakar. In this policy "we" and "us" mean the people who run {{ app_name }}. You can reach us at {{ mentors[0].email }}. That address also receives privacy requests and complaints.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the site needs to work.

  • Account details. Your email address, your name and profile photo if you sign in with Google, and a unique account ID. If you sign up with email and password, your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see it.
  • Your learning data. The tests you take (which questions, your answers, marks, time per question), your study sessions, study plan, tasks and reminder settings.
  • Technical data. Your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. Our hosting provider records these in routine server logs. We use them to keep the site running and to stop abuse.
  • Abuse-prevention signals. Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (through Firebase App Check) checks that requests come from our real website. Google collects device and browser signals for this.

We do not collect payment details, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, or anything from your device that the site does not need.

3. Why we use it (and our basis for doing so)

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To score your tests, show your progress and weak topics, and sync them across your devices.
  • To protect the site from misuse and to fix errors.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We process this data with your consent, which you give when you accept these policies, and for the legitimate uses listed above.

4. Where your data lives and who else handles it

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, App Check / reCAPTCHA). Your account and synced learning data are stored in a Google Firestore database that only your own account can read or change.
  • Google Drive. Books and notes open in Google's Drive viewer. Google may set its own cookies when that viewer loads.
  • Vercel. Our website is hosted on Vercel, which handles requests and keeps server logs.

These providers may process data on servers outside India. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, and we do not share your data with anyone else except when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning data until you delete your account. Server logs kept by our hosting provider are deleted on their normal schedule.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything. Open your profile and choose delete account. This removes your account, your synced tests and your study data, and clears the copy stored in your browser.
  • Clear this device only. Use "Clear history on this device" on the Progress page, or clear your browser's site data.
  • Access, correct or withdraw. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Complaints. Write to the address in section 1 first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, once that Board is operating.

7. Children

{{ app_name }} is meant for adults and for students preparing for GATE. If you are under 18, please use it with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian.

8. Security

Sign-in checks are done by Google. Your session is kept in a protected cookie that scripts on the page cannot read. No system is perfectly secure, so if you find a security problem, please email us.
